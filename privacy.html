Privacy Policy

FELIX (the "App") · last updated 31 October 2026

The App is a private, single-user tool: the person who runs it (the "Operator") publishes their own videos to their own accounts. It has no other users and does not collect data about anyone else.

1. What data the App handles

  • Access and refresh tokens issued by the platform when the Operator connects their own account.
  • Basic identifiers of that account: open_id, nickname, username, and from TikTok's creator information (and the user id and username of connected Instagram and X accounts) the privacy options, interaction settings and maximum video length the account allows.
  • The Operator's own videos and titles, and a local list of what was sent and when (publish identifiers), so that nothing is sent twice.

The App collects nothing about viewers, followers, commenters or any other person, and does not read the account's videos, messages or contacts.

2. Where it is stored

Only on the Operator's own private server, in files readable by the Operator alone.

3. Who it is shared with

Nobody. Tokens and videos are sent only to the platform concerned, to perform the posting the Operator asked for. No analytics, cookies, advertising or tracking; no data is sold, rented or shared.

4. How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until the Operator disconnects the account or they expire. The local list of sent videos is kept for the Operator's own records.

5. Disconnecting and deleting

The Operator can revoke the App's access at any time in the platform's settings for connected apps, and can delete the stored tokens from the App.

6. Children

The App is not directed to anyone other than its Operator and collects no personal data from children or anyone else.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
