FELIX (the "App") · last updated 31 October 2026

1. What the App is

The App is a private publishing tool run by a single individual (the "Operator") on the Operator's own server. Its only purpose is to publish the Operator's own original short videos (chess lessons from The Chess Why, safety tips from Sang-froid, and the animated series Fort Hope from Felix Alive) to social media accounts that the Operator owns and controls.

2. Who may use it

Nobody else. The App has no public sign-up and no other users. The only accounts ever connected are the Operator's own, authorised by the Operator through the platform's own login screen.

3. How it uses TikTok

The App uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish, to:

  • identify the connected account (nickname, username) and show which account will receive a post;
  • publish one video at a time, only after the Operator has reviewed it on the "Post to TikTok" page, chosen its privacy level and interaction settings, and clicked Post.

It never reads, comments, follows or messages on behalf of anyone, and never acts on another person's content. The App is used in accordance with TikTok's Terms of Service, Developer Terms and Community Guidelines.

3b. Instagram and X

The App also publishes the same videos to the Operator's own Instagram accounts (Instagram API with Instagram Login, content publishing only) and X accounts (X API v2, OAuth 2.0 scopes tweet.read, tweet.write, users.read, media.write, offline.access), to post a video with its caption. It never likes, follows, replies, sends messages or reads other users' data.

4. Content

Only videos created and owned by the Operator are published. The Operator is solely responsible for what is published.
